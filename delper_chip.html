Section I — Heterogeneous Silicon Architecture Overview

To support the 21-year roadmap of the Delper Kindness Architecture (DKA) across 6.0 billion deployment nodes, Delper Global Holding Company (DGHC) implements a dual-silicon strategy. Rather than deploying a single monolithic processor across all nodes — which creates unsustainable thermal, power, and manufacturing yield bottlenecks — the architecture splits the workload into two distinct, domain-specific 3nm Gate-All-Around (GAA) ASICs:

  1. THE DUCP-1 (CORE NEXUS): A high-performance, ultra-large-scale datacenter orchestration engine optimized for high-lane CXL bus scheduling, global transport hub routing, and multi-tenant edge execution.
  2. THE DECP-1 (EDGE TERMINAL): An ultra-low-power, highly optimized companion chip designed for local haptic terminal interfaces, sensor telemetry ingestion, and line-rate encryption.

  1. Root-of-Trust (RoT) Ignition: On power-on, the DUCP-1 boots from its immutable on-die ROM, verifying its local firmware stack. Subordinate commercial accelerators (NVIDIA/AMD) are locked in a hard reset state.
